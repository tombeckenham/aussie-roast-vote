Dr. Sophie Scamps (Independent)
Dr. Sophie Scamps is the overachiever we all secretly resent—she’s a former GP, an Olympic athlete, and now Mackellar’s incumbent Independent. Talk about a triple threat! She’s out here prescribing climate action and integrity like they’re Panadol, but after forgetting to declare her own shares and directorship, she might need a dose of her own medicine. Still, you’ve got to hand it to her—she’s running laps around the competition, even if she occasionally trips over her own paperwork.

James Brown (Liberal)
James Brown’s resume reads like an action movie script: army officer, space industry CEO, and even ex-son-in-law to a Prime Minister. Is he running for MP or auditioning for James Bond? He’s promising to tackle the cost of living, but with his space gig, he’s probably more likely to launch Mackellar into orbit than lower your grocery bill. And with that military background, he’ll have Parliament saluting in no time—though good luck getting them to march in a straight line, mate.

Jeffrey Quinn (Labor)
Jeffrey Quinn’s spent over 35 years in education, so he’s basically the teacher who’d give you detention for breathing too loud. He’s all about fairness and community service—think of him as Mackellar’s hall monitor, keeping everyone in check. After missing out in Pittwater in 2023, he’s back for round two. Is he after a gold star or just loves the grind? Either way, Jeff’s ready to grade Parliament’s homework, and he’s not handing out participation trophies.

Ethan Hrnjak (Greens)
At just 23, Ethan Hrnjak is the baby of the bunch—probably still learning how to sort his bins properly. He’s all fired up about climate change and social justice, which is fair enough, but mate, have you even had time to enjoy a snag at a Bunnings barbecue? He’s out saving the planet one rally at a time, but let’s be real—Ethan’s so green, he might sprout leaves before he wins a seat.

Lisa Cotton (Independent)
Lisa Cotton’s the community mum we didn’t know we needed. With 30 years in social enterprise and a heart for domestic violence support, she’s tougher than a burnt snag. She’s so in tune with Mackellar, she probably knows every dog’s birthday. Lisa’s all about empowerment, but if she wins, Parliament might turn into a group hug session—just don’t expect her to bake cookies for the crossbench.

Brad Hayman (One Nation)
Brad Hayman’s flying the One Nation flag, so he’s all about “Aussie-first” vibes. He’d probably build a moat around the Northern Beaches if he could. His big pitch is citizen-initiated referenda—imagine voting on whether to fix potholes or ban avocado toast. Brad’s pushing for housing and energy reform, but let’s be honest, he’s more likely to lead the charge at the pub trivia night than in Canberra.

Justin Addison (Libertarian)
Justin Addison’s a Libertarian, meaning he wants government so tiny, it’d fit in your glovebox. He’s all about personal freedom—probably thinks speed limits are just friendly suggestions. His party wants to axe departments like Education, which is bold, but mate, how’s the postie meant to deliver your mail? Justin’s promising tax cuts and less red tape, but he’s more likely to cut his own bangs than Parliament’s bureaucracy.

Amber Robertson (Trumpet of Patriots)
Amber Robertson’s with Trumpet of Patriots, a party so patriotic they’d have you saluting your cereal. Tied to Clive Palmer, they’ve spent more on dodgy ‘90s ads than actual ideas. Amber’s pushing conservative values, but with that name, she’s probably just here to toot her own horn. Patriotism’s great, but maybe ease up on the trumpet solos before breakfast, yeah?

Mandeep Singh (Independent)
Mandeep “Sunny” Singh is the café owner turned councillor turned Independent. After ditching the Liberals, he’s like the barista who said, “Stuff it, I’ll brew my own.” He’s promising to shake up Mackellar, but he’s more likely to perfect your cappuccino than rock Canberra. Sunny’s all about local vibes, and with no party behind him, he’s either got guts or just really loves a solo gig. Chuck us a coffee with that campaign, mate!
